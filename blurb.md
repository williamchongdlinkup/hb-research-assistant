# HB Research Assistant — Link Blurb

The HB Research Assistant is a newly enhanced research tool from Nan Tien Institute for the study of Humanistic Buddhism. It draws on decades of curated scholarship, nearly 1,900 bibliography entries, and brings three things together in one place. Smart search lets you ask in plain English and see exactly how your request was interpreted, then fine-tune it. Grounded Q&A lets you ask a research question and get an answer drawn only from the corpus, with citations you can verify. And a bibliography audit checks your own reference list against the collection to flag what is verified, missing, or worth adding. Results export in RIS, BibTeX, and formatted Chicago/APA/MLA citations. Free to use at https://hb-research-assistant-production.up.railway.app/, it accompanies the interactive bibliography visualiser at visualiser.nantien.edu.au.
